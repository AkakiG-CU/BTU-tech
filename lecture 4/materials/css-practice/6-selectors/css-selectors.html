Selector Specificity Reminder

Remember the specificity hierarchy:

  1. Inline styles: style="..." (1000 points)
  2. IDs: #header (100 points)
  3. Classes, attributes, pseudo-classes: .box, [type="email"], :hover (10 points)
  4. Elements and pseudo-elements: p, ::before (1 point)
