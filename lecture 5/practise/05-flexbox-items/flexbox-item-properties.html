Summary of Flex Item Properties:
  • order: -∞ to +∞ (controls visual order)
  • flex-grow: 0+ (how much to grow)
  • flex-shrink: 0+ (how much to shrink)
  • flex-basis: auto, 0, length, percentage (initial size)
  • flex: shorthand for grow shrink basis
